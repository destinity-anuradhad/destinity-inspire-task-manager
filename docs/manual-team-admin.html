1. Your Role

As a Team Admin, you manage your team's members, sprints, and settings. You have full control over your team's tasks and can create new users directly into your team. You do not have access to other teams or system-wide user management.

2. Logging In

  1. Open the app in your browser.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Complete the reCAPTCHA check.
  4. Click Login.

If you are a member of only one team, you are taken directly to the dashboard. If you belong to multiple teams, you will be prompted to select a team first.

3. Switching Teams

If you are a member of more than one team, click the team name in the top navigation bar. A dropdown shows your available teams. Click any team to switch — you stay on the same page but the data switches to that team.

5. Team Management (/Teams/Manage)

Click your team name in the top nav → select your team → click "Team Settings" if shown. Alternatively navigate to /Teams/Manage/{id}.

Editing Team Details

  • Name — update the team's display name.
  • Description — short description of the team's purpose.
  • Color — team brand colour used in badges and charts.

Click Save Changes.

Adding an Existing User

  1. Select their name from the Add Member dropdown.
  2. Choose a Team Role (Member, Team Lead, Team Admin).
  3. Click Add.

Creating a New User

  1. Click Show / Hide next to "Create New User".
  2. Fill in:
    • Full Name
    • Username — unique login name (lowercase)
    • Email — optional, needed for email notifications
    • Password — minimum 8 characters
    • Team Role — Member or Team Lead (Team Admins cannot create other Team Admins)
  3. Click Create & Add.

Changing Member Roles

Use the role dropdown next to any member's name. You can assign Member, Team Lead, or Team Admin.

Removing Members

Click the × button next to a member to remove them from the team. The user account is not deleted.

6. Sprints (/Sprints)

Creating a Sprint

  1. Go to Sprints in the navigation.
  2. Click New Sprint.
  3. Enter Sprint Name, Start Date, End Date, and optional Goal.
  4. Click Save.

Managing Sprints

  • Set Active — marks the sprint as current. Only one sprint can be active at a time.
  • Complete Sprint — closes the sprint. Incomplete tasks return to the backlog.
  • Delete Sprint — permanently removes the sprint. Tasks are not deleted.
  • Capacity Planning — set each team member's daily available hours.

Creating a Task

  1. Click New Task.
  2. Required: Goal and Project.
  3. Fill in: Client, Owner, Priority, Status, Sprint, Target Date, Item Type, Parent Task, Weight, Labels, Notes, Acceptance Criteria.
  4. Click Save.

Recycle Bin

As a Team Admin you can view deleted tasks. Contact a SysAdmin to restore or permanently delete tasks.

Bulk Operations

Select multiple tasks via checkboxes: Change Status, Change Owner, Assign Sprint, Change Priority, Delete.

8. Dashboard

My Dashboard

Personal task counts (Total, Done, In Progress, Open, Overdue), completion percentage, recent activity, and best-performing project.

Team Dashboard

Click Team Performance to see: status distribution, project breakdown, owner workload, recent team activity.

9. Charts

  • Velocity — completed story points per sprint
  • Status distribution
  • Priority breakdown (P0–P3)
  • Owner workload
  • Project and property distribution

10. Activity Log

Every change made within your team: who created, edited, or commented on which task, and when.

11. Profile & Notifications

Access via the avatar/name in the top-right corner.

  • Update email — required to receive email notifications.
  • Change password — minimum 8 characters.
  • Notification preferences — assigned tasks, comments, blocked tasks.

The notification bell (🔔) shows unread alerts.
