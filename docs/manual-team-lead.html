1. Your Role

As a Team Lead, you manage day-to-day task work for your team. You can create tasks, assign work to team members, and add new members to your team. You do not manage sprints, team settings, or system users.

2. Logging In

  1. Open the app in your browser.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Complete the reCAPTCHA check.
  4. Click Login.

If you belong to only one team, you are taken directly to the dashboard. If you belong to multiple teams, you will be prompted to select a team first.

3. Switching Teams

If you are a member of more than one team, click the team name in the top navigation bar. A dropdown shows your available teams. Click any team to switch.

5. Adding Team Members

Team Leads can add new member-level users to their team.

Creating a New User

  1. Navigate to /Teams/Manage/{id} (ask your Team Admin for the link if needed).
  2. In the Create New User section, fill in:
    • Full Name
    • Username — unique login name (lowercase)
    • Email — optional
    • Password — minimum 8 characters
  3. Click Create & Add.
Note: New users created by a Team Lead are always added as Member. Team Leads cannot assign Lead or Team Admin roles. Contact your Team Admin to change roles or remove members.

Filtering

Click Show filters to open the filter bar. Filter by keyword, module, client, owner, status, priority, or date range.

Creating a Task

  1. Click New Task.
  2. Required: Goal and Project.
  3. Optional: Client, Owner(s), Priority, Status, Sprint, Target Date, Start Date, Item Type, Parent Task, Weight, Labels, Notes, Acceptance Criteria.
  4. Click Save.

Editing a Task

Click any task to open the canvas. Change any field and click Save.

Deleting Tasks

Open a task and click Delete, or check multiple tasks and use Bulk Delete. Deleted tasks go to the Recycle Bin — contact a SysAdmin or Team Admin to restore.

Bulk Operations

Select multiple tasks via checkboxes: Change Status, Change Owner, Assign Sprint, Change Priority, Delete.

Exporting

Click Export CSV to download the current filtered task list.

7. Sprints (View Only)

Go to Sprints to see current and upcoming sprints, their dates, goals, and assigned tasks. Sprint creation, completion, and deletion are managed by your Team Admin or SysAdmin.

8. Dashboard

My Dashboard

Personal stats: total assigned, done, in progress, open, and overdue tasks. Also shows recent activity and best-performing project.

Team Dashboard

Click Team Performance to see status distribution, project breakdown, owner workload, and recent team activity.

11. Profile & Notifications

Access via the avatar/name in the top-right corner.

  • Update email — required for email notifications.
  • Change password — minimum 8 characters.
  • Notification preferences — assigned tasks, comments, blocked tasks.

The notification bell (🔔) shows unread alerts.

13. What Team Leads Cannot Do

  • Create or manage sprints (set active, complete, delete)
  • Edit team name, colour, or description
  • Remove members from the team
  • Change member roles
  • Access Master Files (system user management)
  • Restore or permanently delete tasks from the Recycle Bin
  • Access other teams' data
